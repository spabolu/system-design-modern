The [curriculum](/docs/intro) is a learning path that starts with the basics and
builds toward advanced systems. Notes is where I keep dated observations about
new system design developments.

<!-- truncate -->

A new paper, an Azure service change, or a useful production write-up can change
how I approach a design. Each note explains what changed and links back to the
curriculum pages it affects.

Notes is not a second textbook. The curriculum holds the explanations I want to
keep current. A note preserves what I thought at a particular time, with sources
and enough context to revisit that judgment later.
